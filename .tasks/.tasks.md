## One region, and the second thing worth saying (2026-09-10)

<!-- Second bullet of "Live-region-mount follow-ups" (2026-09-10) and third of "Row-props follow-ups": "Nothing announces anything except reorder, and the region is named for reorder", and "Nothing announces why the actions vanished". -->

- [DONE: 2026-09-10] [est: 22000] **`lib/announce.{ts,web.ts}` is the door; `reorder-announcement` is a caller** — the live region was called `collectables-reorder-live-region` and lived in `lib/reorder-announcement.web.ts`, which was the right shape while reordering was the only thing the app ever said out loud. The prediction written down that morning was that the next thing worth speaking would "either get a second region or rename this one", and a second assertive region is two readers interrupting each other.

  **`lib/reorder-announcement.ts` has no platform spelling now, and never had a reason for one.** What differed between its two halves was the MECHANISM — `AccessibilityInfo` on native, an ARIA live region on web — and what it actually owns is the decision not to speak (`announcedPosition`: a wrong "position 3 of 7" read aloud is worse than nothing). The mechanism moved to the pair; the decision stayed in one file. `lint:platform-pairs` still sees two pairs, and the deleted spelling is one fewer place for the two halves to drift.

- [DONE: 2026-09-10] [est: 8000] **The reorder gate says why it closed** — `enabled: isDragBranch` removes both move actions from every row, silently. The visible notice is `accessibilityRole="alert"`, which announces itself on Android and on web and does NOT on iOS, where VoiceOver reads an alert only when it happens to reach one; it also sits in the list header, which a user deep in a long collection has scrolled past. So a screen-reader owner taps "Reorder", finds the actions simply absent, and nothing anywhere explains it — the exact "indistinguishable from a broken button" the notice was written for, in the one place the notice cannot reach.

  **The notice's own words, through the same door.** `announceMessage(t("reorderBlockedBySort"))` — two phrasings for one state would be two things to translate and one of them to forget, and the key is already in all six locales. On the transition only, because the value is recomputed every render and announcing on each would interrupt the reader mid-sentence for as long as the sort is on.

  The web suite moved with the module (`reorder-announcement-web.test.ts` → `announce-web.test.ts`) and lost the cases that were never about the DOM: the `announcedPosition` silences are asserted where that function lives and runs, and what is left here is the region. 16 cases there, 5 new ones for the gate announcement, and three cases that pin the split itself — the caller naming neither `AccessibilityInfo` nor `document`, and exactly one live region declared. Verified in `dist/`: `collectables-live-region` is in the entry chunk and the old id is gone. `npm run verify` green: 7988 tests, 20/20 guards, bundle 10.3 KiB under budget. No DB migration, no creds.
